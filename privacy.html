Privacy Policy — LEVLI: Cloud Defense

Effective date: August 21, 2026

The short version: LEVLI: Cloud Defense collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — rank, scores, settings, tutorial state, selected cloud provider, and which question packs are unlocked — is stored only on your device (browser local storage on the web, app storage on iOS). It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's site data removes it permanently.

No network activity

The game runs entirely offline after it loads. It makes no network requests at runtime, and we operate no servers that receive any data from it.

In-app purchases (iOS)

Optional question packs are sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the entire transaction; we never see your payment details, name, or Apple ID. Your purchase is recorded on your device and with your App Store account, which is what the "Restore Purchases" button uses. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The iOS app uses the open-source Capacitor shell to run the game, which makes no network calls of its own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds online features — the updated version will be posted at this page before the change takes effect.
