Privacy Policy — LEVLI: Cloud Defense

Effective date: October 24, 2026

LEVLI is an independent cloud-learning game. You can play without creating a player account. The native iOS version 1 offers 270 free questions and one optional permanent AWS Cloud Practitioner purchase. Production rewarded advertising is disabled in this version.

Progress saved on your device

The game stores scores, rank, preferences, tutorial state, provider selection, selected question pack and an ownership cache on your device. Native iOS uses app storage; the separate web game uses browser storage. The game's progress and adaptive-practice implementation does not upload this local history to a developer-operated progress or analytics server.

Optional adaptive practice stores up to 120 recent answer records: question identifier, correctness and whether an answer timed out. It uses that local history to adjust practice difficulty and topic focus. Turning adaptive practice off stops new adaptive-history entries and returns to classic progression; it does not itself erase existing history.

Purchases handled by Apple

The optional question pack uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment and maintains the account's purchase records. The app verifies eligible ownership and keeps a local ownership cache. The developer does not handle your card details or receive your Apple account password. Product lookup, purchase and restoration communicate with Apple's services. See Apple's privacy policy.

Restore purchases can recover eligible ownership for the purchasing App Store account. It does not synchronize or restore game scores, settings or practice history.

Local records, deletion and backups

Deleting the app or clearing browser site data removes locally stored game progress from that installation. Apple purchase records and device backups are managed separately. Removing local game data does not promise deletion from Apple's records or every backup, and eligible purchased ownership can be restored.

Existing local ad diagnostics may include offers, impressions, earned rewards, revives, timestamps, run and attempt identifiers, and SDK-reported paid values with currency and precision. Test and live totals are separate. The game's local diagnostic implementation does not send these records to a developer-operated analytics server. Deleting local game storage removes these local diagnostic records from that installation.

Third-party libraries and connectivity

The native iOS app uses Capacitor, Apple StoreKit, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform libraries. The Google libraries remain bundled even though production rewarded advertising is disabled for v1. The Release ad integration does not initiate rewarded-ad requests or display ad-consent forms while that feature is disabled. The presence of these libraries is not a promise that all embedded software has no data processing or network activity.

Google documents potential processing of network and approximate-location information, device identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic/performance information by its mobile advertising SDK. See Google's iOS SDK data disclosure and Google's advertising privacy information. Any future activation of production advertising will require a separate reviewed release and policy update.

Native game assets are bundled. The separate hosted browser beta may offer different content or unlock behavior. It downloads assets when it loads; its hosting service and your network handle those requests. The browser beta is separate from the native App Store purchase flow. If you choose a share action or open an external link, the selected app or website handles that action under its own practices.

Contacting support

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to the request. Email is handled by the services used by you and the support address. For privacy questions or questions about support correspondence, contact jermaine.ragsdale@me.com. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Changes and independent learning material

Updates to this policy will describe changes in the released app's practices. LEVLI is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud. Practice material does not guarantee certification results.
