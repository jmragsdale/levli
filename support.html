LEVLI: Cloud Defense support

This page describes the native iOS v1 app. The separate browser beta may have different content or unlock behavior.

For app problems, question corrections or purchase-unlock issues, email jermaine.ragsdale@me.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. A question identifier or screenshot can help with a content correction. Do not send account passwords or payment-card details.

Free play and optional packs

Free play includes 270 questions across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. Three optional packs are available: AWS Cloud Practitioner Pack, Azure AI Prep and Google Cloud AI Prep. Each contains 100 practice questions and is a separate permanent, one-time purchase that also unlocks Runner mode. Buying one pack does not unlock the other banks. There is no subscription. The App Store purchase sheet shows the price for your storefront.

Azure AI Prep and Google Cloud AI Prep share 66 question prompts, for 134 distinct prompts across those two packs. They are not 200 distinct prompts.

Restore purchases

Use Restore purchases on the main menu while signed into the App Store account that bought the packs. An internet connection may be needed. Restore purchases recovers eligible pack ownership; it does not restore scores, practice history or settings. If ownership does not return, email support with the app version and what you see. Apple handles billing and eligible refunds: Apple refund help.

Progress and practice history

Scores, preferences and practice history are saved locally. Deleting the app or clearing browser site data removes progress from that installation. Apple purchase records and device backups are managed separately; deleting local game data does not erase those records.

Advertising in v1

This iOS release does not include advertising or advertising/consent SDKs. You do not need to watch an ad to play. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform libraries and their unused integration have been removed. See the privacy policy for details.
