LEVLI: Cloud Defense support

This page describes the native iOS v1 app. The separate browser beta may have different content or unlock behavior.

For app problems, question corrections or purchase-unlock issues, email jermaine.ragsdale@me.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. A question identifier or screenshot can help with a content correction. Do not send account passwords or payment-card details.

Free play and the optional pack

Free play includes 270 questions across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. The optional AWS Cloud Practitioner pack adds 100 questions and unlocks Runner mode. It is a permanent, one-time purchase; there is no subscription. The App Store purchase sheet shows the price for your storefront.

Restore your purchase

Use Restore purchases on the main menu while signed into the App Store account that bought the pack. An internet connection may be needed. Restore purchases recovers eligible pack ownership; it does not restore scores, practice history or settings. If ownership does not return, email support with the app version and what you see. Apple handles billing and eligible refunds: Apple refund help.

Progress and practice history

Scores, preferences and practice history are saved locally. Deleting the app or clearing browser site data removes progress from that installation. Apple purchase records and device backups are managed separately; deleting local game data does not erase those records.

Advertising in v1

Production rewarded advertising is disabled in this version. You do not need to watch an ad to play. This support page does not offer rewarded-ad benefits or promise that every embedded library has no data processing; see the privacy policy for the retained third-party libraries.
