For app problems, question corrections or purchase-unlock issues, email jermaine.ragsdale@me.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. A question identifier or screenshot can help with a content correction. Do not send account passwords or payment-card details.

Free play and optional packs

Free play includes 270 questions across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud: 90 per provider. The corrected iOS release offers AWS Cloud Practitioner Pack, Azure Fundamentals Prep and Google Cloud Digital Leader Prep. Each contains 100 practice questions and is a separate permanent, one-time purchase that also unlocks Runner mode. Buying one pack does not unlock the other banks. There is no subscription. Each pack is US $2.99, with United States availability only. The App Store purchase sheet shows the actual price when a product is available.

The Azure Fundamentals and Cloud Digital Leader questions are newly authored from official documentation. Shared concepts can recur across providers. Practice material is not an official exam or a complete exam-preparation course.

Restore purchases and legacy ownership

Use Restore purchases on the main menu while signed into the App Store account that bought the packs. An internet connection may be needed. Restore purchases recovers eligible ownership; it does not restore scores, practice history or settings. If ownership does not return, email support with the app version and what you see. Apple handles billing and eligible refunds: Apple refund help.

Earlier AI pack identities remain recognized for verified eligible restoration and Runner access. Legacy AI ownership does not grant Azure Fundamentals Prep or Google Cloud Digital Leader Prep. The legacy AI banks are not offered for sale or available for selection in the corrected iOS release. This preserves recognition of verified purchases without changing which question bank a purchase owns.

Progress and practice history

Scores, preferences and practice history are saved locally. Deleting the app or clearing browser site data removes progress from that installation. Apple purchase records and device backups are managed separately; deleting local game data does not erase those records.

Advertising in v1

This iOS release does not include advertising or advertising/consent SDKs. You do not need to watch an ad to play. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform libraries and their unused integration have been removed. See the privacy policy for details.
